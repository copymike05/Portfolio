Author Website

Joseph Muller


Status: In progress

I'm currently building a custom website for Joseph Muller, a self-published author of historical fiction novels who recently published his first children's story.

This project involves:

  • Designing a custom site that reflects his personal brand
  • Bringing all of his books together in one place
  • Linking each book directly to its respective Amazon page
  • Featuring his podcast interviews so readers can hear from him directly

Since Amazon already prints and ships his books, a custom website was the right fit. This gives him a professional home for his work without changing how his books get sold.

Check back soon for updates.
