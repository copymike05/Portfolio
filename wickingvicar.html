Wicking Vicar


Status: Completed

The above video shows upgrades I made for Wicking Vicar, an established Shopify store selling performance clergy shirts, children's toys, and necklaces.

The Home and About Us pages each featured the same video, uploaded as a file into Shopify's admin. The file was oversized, making it prone to buffering once played. While reviewing the site, I also found the Home page was displaying the video twice and the About Us page had it running on a continuous loop. Rather than work around a file that size, I suggested hosting the video on YouTube and embedding it instead.

This project involved...

  • Removing the oversized video from both pages, including the duplicate on the home page
  • Building a custom Video with Text section that streams the video from YouTube. This video plays once rather than looping
  • Moving the About Us content from the Home page and onto the About Us page

The result is a cleaner site with a video section that fits the flow of the store rather than interrupting it. The client and I have more updates planned, which I'll add here once they go live.
