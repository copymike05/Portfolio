Shopify Customizations

Wicking Vicar


Status:Customizations Complete

The above video shows upgrades to Wicking Vicar, an established Shopify store selling performance clergy shirts, children's toys, and necklaces.

The Home and About Us pages each featured the same video uploaded as a file in Shopify's admin. It was oversized and prone to buffering. I also discovered the Home page displayed the video twice while the About Us page ran it on a non-stop loop. Rather than compressing a file that size, I suggested hosting and embedding it on YouTube.

This project involved...

  • Removing the oversized video from both pages, including the duplicate on the home page
  • Building a custom Video with Text section that streams the video from YouTube. This video plays once rather than looping
  • Moving the About Us content from the Home page to the About Us page

The site is now cleaner and the video section flows better. The client and I have more upgrades planned, which I'll include here once they're live.
